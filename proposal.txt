# StudyStreak MY Project Proposal

## 1. Project Title

**StudyStreak MY: A Gamified Sejarah Learning Application for Malaysian Secondary-School Students**

## 2. Project Overview

StudyStreak MY is a mobile learning application designed to help Malaysian secondary-school students study Sejarah more consistently and effectively.


The application will provide Sejarah learning content for students from **Form 1 to Form 5**. Students will be able to access simplified notes, complete practice quizzes, manage their Sejarah homework, maintain a daily study streak and monitor their learning progress.

To support students from different language backgrounds, the notes will be available in:

* English
* Bahasa Melayu
* Chinese

The application will be developed using **React Native**, allowing it to run on both Android and iOS devices.

## 3. Problem Statement

Many Malaysian secondary-school students find Sejarah difficult because the subject contains many facts, names, dates, events and explanations that must be understood and remembered.

Some common problems faced by students include:

* Difficulty understanding long textbook explanations
* Difficulty identifying important facts
* Forgetting historical dates, events and individuals
* Lack of suitable practice questions
* Difficulty studying consistently
* Forgetting homework and revision tasks
* Difficulty understanding Sejarah in only one language
* Studying only when an examination is approaching

Although textbooks and revision books are available, students may still find the content complicated or difficult to organise.

Students therefore need a simple mobile application that helps them study Sejarah regularly, understand important topics and test their knowledge.

## 4. Proposed Solution

StudyStreak MY will combine Sejarah learning materials, homework management, quizzes and gamification in one application.

Students will be able to:

1. Select their school Form.
2. Select a Sejarah chapter.
3. Choose their preferred notes language.
4. Read simplified chapter notes.
5. View important points and summaries.
6. Complete chapter-based quizzes.
7. Review correct and incorrect answers.
8. Add and manage Sejarah homework.
9. Build a daily study streak.
10. Monitor their chapter and quiz progress.

The application will encourage students to complete at least one learning activity every day.

## 5. Target Users

The main users are Malaysian secondary-school students aged approximately 13 to 17.

The application will support:

* Form 1 students
* Form 2 students
* Form 3 students
* Form 4 students
* Form 5 students

The initial users may come from:

* Government secondary schools
* Private schools
* Tuition centres
* Homeschooling programmes
* Online learning programmes

The first testing group may consist of classmates, schoolmates, friends and students from tuition centres.

## 6. Estimated Number of Users

During the first testing stage, the application will be tested by approximately:

**10 to 30 students**

After the application is launched, the first target is:

**100 registered users**

If the application receives positive feedback and more content is added, the long-term target may increase to:

**500 to 1,000 Malaysian secondary-school students**

These figures are project targets and are not guaranteed user numbers.

## 7. Main Features

### 7.1 User Registration and Login

Students can create an account using their email and password.

The application will save:

* Student name
* Email
* Selected Form
* Current study streak
* Best study streak
* Quiz results
* Chapter progress
* Homework tasks

Students will remain logged in until they choose to log out.

### 7.2 Form Selection

Students can select their school level before accessing the learning content.

The available levels are:

* Form 1
* Form 2
* Form 3
* Form 4
* Form 5

After selecting a Form, the application will display the relevant Sejarah chapters.

Students may change their selected Form through the profile page.

### 7.3 Sejarah Chapter Library

Each Form will contain a list of Sejarah chapters based on the Malaysian secondary-school syllabus.

The chapter page will include:

* Chapter title
* Learning progress
* Simplified notes
* Important points
* Practice quiz
* Quick test
* Quiz history

For the first development version, only selected chapters may be fully completed. More chapters can be added gradually after the application structure is working correctly.

### 7.4 Multilingual Notes

Students can choose the language they prefer before opening a chapter’s notes.

The available languages are:

* English
* Bahasa Melayu
* Chinese

The student can select a language from the language-selection page.

The notes will include:

* Topic introduction
* Important historical events
* Important individuals
* Important dates
* Keywords
* Main facts
* Short explanations
* Chapter summary

Students may also switch between languages while reading the notes.

The translations should explain the same historical content clearly without changing the meaning of the original topic.

### 7.5 Important Points

Each chapter will contain a separate important-points section.

This section will show the most important information that students should understand or remember.

For example:

* Important rulers
* Important historical events
* Causes of an event
* Effects of an event
* Government systems
* Economic activities
* Social structures
* Important agreements
* Important dates

This feature will help students revise a chapter quickly before a test or examination.

### 7.6 Practice Quiz

Students can complete quizzes after reading the notes.

Version 1 will mainly use multiple-choice questions.

Each question will contain:

* One question
* Four answer choices
* One correct answer
* A short explanation

The quiz may contain five or ten questions.

After completing the quiz, students will see:

* Total score
* Percentage
* Correct answers
* Incorrect answers
* Answer explanations
* Retry Quiz button
* Return to Chapter button

Completing a quiz will contribute to the student’s daily study streak.

### 7.7 Review Answers

Students can review their answers after completing a quiz.

The review page will display:

* The original question
* The student’s answer
* The correct answer
* Whether the answer was correct or incorrect
* A short explanation

This feature allows students to learn from their mistakes instead of only seeing their final score.

### 7.8 Homework and Task Manager

Students can add Sejarah homework and revision tasks.

Each task can include:

* Task title
* Description
* Due date
* Priority
* Completion status
* Optional image

Examples of tasks include:

* Complete Sejarah Chapter 2 exercise
* Revise Chapter 4
* Prepare Sejarah presentation
* Finish teacher’s worksheet
* Complete a chapter quiz

The student can:

* Add a task
* Edit a task
* Delete a task
* Mark a task as completed
* View pending tasks
* View completed tasks

### 7.9 Daily Study Streak

The streak system records how many consecutive days the student completes at least one valid learning activity.

A valid activity includes:

* Completing a Sejarah quiz
* Completing a homework task
* Completing a chapter-learning activity
* Uploading evidence of completed homework

The streak will follow these rules:

* The first completed activity starts a one-day streak.
* Completing an activity the following day increases the streak.
* Several activities completed on the same day only increase the streak once.
* Missing one complete day resets the current streak.
* The best streak will remain saved.
* Opening the application without completing an activity will not increase the streak.

The streak page will display:

* Current streak
* Best streak
* Whether today’s activity has been completed
* Activity during the previous seven days

### 7.10 Progress Tracking

Students can view their learning progress.

The progress page will display:

* Number of quizzes completed
* Average quiz score
* Current streak
* Best streak
* Chapters started
* Chapters completed
* Progress for each chapter

Simple progress bars can be used to show how much of each chapter has been completed.

Advanced graphs are not required for the first version.

### 7.11 Profile Page

The profile page will display:

* Student name
* Selected Form
* Current streak
* Best streak
* Number of completed quizzes
* Achievements
* Homework history
* Quiz history

Students can also:

* Edit their profile
* Change their selected Form
* Change application settings
* Enable or disable notifications
* View help information
* Log out

## 16. Risks and Solutions

### Risk 1: Too Much Learning Content

Preparing notes for every Form and chapter in three languages requires a large amount of work.

**Solution:** Begin with one or two complete chapters for each Form and add more chapters gradually.

### Risk 2: Translation Errors

Historical facts may be translated incorrectly or lose their original meaning.

**Solution:** Check all three language versions carefully and ensure they contain the same meaning.

### Risk 3: Incorrect Sejarah Information

Incorrect names, dates or events may affect students’ learning.

**Solution:** Check all notes and questions against reliable learning materials before publishing them.

### Risk 4: Streak Calculation Errors

The streak may update incorrectly because of date or time-zone differences.

**Solution:** Calculate streaks using the Malaysian local date and test missed-day situations carefully.

### Risk 5: Copyright Problems

Uploading full textbooks or copyrighted examination papers may create legal issues.

**Solution:** Create original summaries and original practice questions. Do not reproduce entire textbooks or examination papers.

### Risk 6: Project Becomes Too Difficult

Developing all planned features at the same time may delay completion.

**Solution:** Complete the login, chapter, notes, quiz and streak systems first. Add optional features only after the main application works.

## 19. Project Summary

StudyStreak MY is a React Native mobile application designed to help Malaysian secondary-school students study Sejarah from Form 1 to Form 5.

Students can select their Form, choose a chapter, read notes in English, Bahasa Melayu or Chinese, complete quizzes, manage homework and build a daily study streak.

The first release will focus on a limited number of fully prepared chapters while supporting the complete Form 1 to Form 5 application structure.

By combining multilingual learning content, task management, quizzes and gamification, StudyStreak MY aims to make Sejarah more understandable, organised and engaging for Malaysian students.
