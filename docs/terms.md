# Terms of Use

StudyStreak MY is an independent Sejarah study aid for revision, homework tracking, quizzes, and study streaks. It is not an official KPM or DBP application.

AI summaries and answers are optional study support. They can be incomplete or incorrect, so students should check their textbook, teacher guidance, and classroom notes.

Users must be at least 13 years old to create an account and use AI features. Do not enter private personal information into AI chat.

The app may change or remove features while it is being prepared for public release.
