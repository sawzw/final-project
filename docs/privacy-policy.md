# Privacy Policy

StudyStreak MY collects the information needed to run a student study account: email address, display name, school form, language and theme preferences, homework tasks, quiz attempts, streak progress, completed notes, and AI chat history.

When Supabase is configured, account authentication and study progress are stored in Supabase. Optional AI summaries and questions are sent to a Supabase Edge Function, which calls Google Gemini. The Gemini API key is kept on the server and is not stored on the device.

StudyStreak MY does not sell student data. Data is used to provide login, progress sync, learning history, and optional AI study help.

Users can delete their account in the Profile settings. Account deletion removes the Supabase Auth account and associated StudyStreak MY app data.
